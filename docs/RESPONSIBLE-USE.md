# Responsible use

Roulette JARVIS is an entertainment and portfolio project. It is designed to make its mathematics visible, not to imply that roulette history predicts future independent spins.

## Product commitments

The interface must:

- state that recent clustering does not alter the next-spin odds;
- label hit probability as ticket coverage, not confidence;
- show total stake, maximum planned exposure, and budget remaining;
- require an entertainment budget, chip value, and maximum spins;
- prevent confirmation when planned exposure exceeds the budget;
- show result-dependent returns for mixed tickets;
- allow the user to finish a session early;
- end the session when its preselected limit is reached.

The interface must not:

- describe a number as “due”;
- claim a guaranteed or reliable prediction;
- offer Martingale or loss-recovery progression;
- automatically increase stakes after a loss;
- encourage chasing losses;
- integrate with real-money betting or automate casino interaction;
- hide the house edge or present selective winning outcomes.

## Mathematical context

On a standard European roulette wheel, a straight or split bet carries the same house edge of approximately 2.70% when standard rules apply. Changing bet combinations changes variance and coverage, not the underlying expected value.

## Personal safeguards

- Treat the session budget as spent entertainment money.
- Never gamble with money needed for essentials.
- Do not borrow to gamble.
- Stop when the selected time, spin, or money limit is reached.
- If gambling stops feeling recreational, do not use the app.

## Scope

The project is not financial advice, a gambling service, or a system for beating roulette. Users remain responsible for following local law, venue rules, and age restrictions.
